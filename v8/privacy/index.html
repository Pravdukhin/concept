What we count

To understand which sections are read and which buttons are pressed, we keep anonymous visit statistics with GoatCounter. It records the pages opened and the buttons pressed, the site or app the visitor came from, country and region, browser, operating system, language and screen width. The IP address is used only to find the country and region and is not stored. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
