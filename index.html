About

Hi! I’m Yuriy, and I’ve been in technical event production for over 20 years, in recent years more and more often around the world. That usually means 60 flights a year, and yes, I love planes too.

I also love bringing structure to chaos and building a system in everything I take on.

As a technical director, I specialise in conferences and high-end private events. As my portfolio shows, galas and stadium projects come my way too.

In summer I live near Milan, in winter I’m often in Dubai. I work on projects in SketchUp and Vectorworks, and we use visualisation tools built on Unreal Engine. For each project I bring together a trusted team of specialists, and I’m personally accountable for the result.

I work in English and Russian.
