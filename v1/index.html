How I work

First the project is developed, then it is built. The client sees what they pay for before anyone travels, and the budget is agreed faster.

  1. Venue inspection and laser scan

    I visit the venue with a 3D scanner and capture every wall, tree and step to the centimetre. Two scanners, in Italy and in the UAE.

  2. 3D model in exact dimensions

    The whole setup is designed on the scan: stage, screens, rigging, light, sound and seating. You can walk through it long before load-in.

  3. Renders and technical drawings

    The client approves the picture, suppliers get drawings instead of guesses. What is drawn is what can be built.

  4. Delivery on site

    I choose and manage the suppliers, run the build and stay with the show until the last guest leaves.
